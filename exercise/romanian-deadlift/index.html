Romanian Deadlift

A hip-hinge movement emphasizing the hamstrings and glutes.

Upper legs · Barbell · Intermediate

How to do the Romanian Deadlift

  1. Stand holding a barbell at hip level with an overhand grip.
  2. Hinge at the hips, pushing them back while keeping legs nearly straight.
  3. Lower the bar along your legs until you feel a hamstring stretch.
  4. Drive your hips forward to return to standing.
  5. Repeat.

Form tips

  • Keep your spine neutral and avoid rounding the lower back.
  • Maintain the same slight knee bend for the entire rep.
  • Go only as deep as your hamstring flexibility allows.
