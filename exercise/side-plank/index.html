Side Plank

A lateral plank variation targeting the obliques and lateral core.

Core · Bodyweight · Beginner

Muscles worked by the Side Plank

Primary

ObliquesOblique AbdominalsDeep CoreTransversus Abdominis

Secondary

Glute MediusGluteus MediusAbsRectus Abdominis

Also called: oblique, obliques, side abs, external oblique, internal oblique, transverse abdominis.

Equipment

No equipment — this is a bodyweight exercise.

How to do the Side Plank

  1. Lie on your side with your forearm on the floor, elbow under your shoulder.
  2. Lift your hips off the floor, forming a straight line from head to feet.
  3. Hold the position, keeping your core tight.
  4. Breathe steadily.
  5. Repeat on both sides.

Form tips

  • Don't let your top shoulder roll forward during the hold.
  • Push the floor away through the forearm rather than sinking into the shoulder.
  • Keep your head in line with your spine.
