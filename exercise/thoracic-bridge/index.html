Thoracic Bridge

A mobility hold that lifts the hips from a tabletop position and opens the chest, extending the thoracic spine while the glutes and shoulders hold the bridge.

Core · Bodyweight · Intermediate

Muscles worked by the Thoracic Bridge

Primary

Lower BackErector SpinaeGlutesGluteus Maximus

Secondary

Rear DeltsPosterior DeltoidTricepsTriceps Brachii

Also called: erector spinae, spinal erectors, lower back muscles, back extensors, glute, glutes.

Equipment

No equipment — this is a bodyweight exercise.

How to do the Thoracic Bridge

  1. Sit on the floor with knees bent and hands behind you, fingers pointing back.
  2. Press through your hands and feet to lift your hips off the floor.
  3. Continue pressing until your chest opens and your arms are extended.
  4. Drop your head back and hold the position.
  5. Breathe steadily while maintaining the bridge.
  6. Lower back down under control.

Form tips

  • Drive the hips high and squeeze the glutes to protect the lower back.
  • Keep the feet flat and the weight even between hands and feet.
