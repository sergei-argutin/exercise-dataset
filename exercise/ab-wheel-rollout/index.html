Ab Wheel Rollout

A core exercise rolling a wheel forward and back, training anti-extension of the spine.

Core · Ab Wheel · Intermediate

Equipment

Ab Wheel

Also called: ab roller, ab wheel roller, power wheel.

How to do the Ab Wheel Rollout

  1. Kneel on the floor and grip the ab wheel handles shoulder-width.
  2. Brace the core hard so the lower back stays neutral.
  3. Roll the wheel slowly forward, extending the body as far as you can control.
  4. Stop before the hips sag or the back arches.
  5. Pull the wheel back to the knees by contracting the abs.

Form tips

  • Brace the core before each rep — do not let the lower back arch.
  • Build range gradually, stopping short of any hip sag.
  • Drop to a wall stop if you cannot control the bottom.
