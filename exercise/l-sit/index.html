L Sit

An advanced static hold with legs extended parallel to the floor, demanding hip flexor and core strength.

Full body · Dip Station · Advanced

Equipment

Dip Station

Also called: parallel bars, dip bars, parallettes, dip stand, p-bars.

How to do the L Sit

  1. Sit on the floor or press up on parallel bars with arms straight.
  2. Lock your elbows and depress your shoulders.
  3. Lift your legs straight out in front of you parallel to the floor.
  4. Form an L shape with your body.
  5. Hold the position for the prescribed duration.
  6. Lower your legs with control to exit.

Form tips

  • Bend your knees into a tuck position if the full hold is too hard.
  • Point your toes and squeeze your quads to keep legs straight.
  • Breathe steadily during the hold rather than holding your breath.
