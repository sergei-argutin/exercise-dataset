Glute Bridge

A bodyweight glute activation exercise performed lying on the floor.

Upper legs · Bodyweight · Beginner

Muscles worked by the Glute Bridge

Primary

GlutesGluteus Maximus

Secondary

HamstringsHamstring Muscles

Also called: glute, glutes, gluteus maximus, buttocks, butt.

Equipment

No equipment — this is a bodyweight exercise.

How to do the Glute Bridge

  1. Lie on your back with knees bent and feet flat on the floor.
  2. Drive your hips up by squeezing your glutes.
  3. Hold briefly at the top with hips fully extended.
  4. Lower your hips back down with control.
  5. Repeat.

Form tips

  • Push through your heels rather than your toes.
  • Avoid arching your lower back at the top.
  • Don't let your knees cave inward as you lift.
