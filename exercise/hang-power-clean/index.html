Hang Power Clean

A clean from the hang caught in a partial squat, building explosive power without dropping into a full squat.

Full body · Barbell · Intermediate

How to do the Hang Power Clean

  1. Stand with feet hip-width apart holding a barbell at your thighs.
  2. Hinge at the hips to lower the bar to just above the knees.
  3. Drive through your hips and legs explosively.
  4. Pull the bar up and catch it on your front shoulders in a partial squat.
  5. Stand tall with the bar racked on your shoulders.
  6. Lower the bar back to the hang position.

Form tips

  • Drive through your hips and legs before the arms pull.
  • Receive the bar with your thighs above parallel.
  • Keep your back flat as you hinge to the start position.
