Single-Arm Dumbbell Overhead Tricep Extension

A one-arm overhead tricep extension giving a long, unilateral stretch of the long head and training anti-side-bend core stability.

Upper arms · Dumbbell · Beginner

Equipment

Dumbbell

Also called: db, dumbell, hex dumbbell, rubber dumbbell, hand weight.

How to do the Single-Arm Dumbbell Overhead Tricep Extension

  1. Stand or sit holding a dumbbell in one hand overhead, arm fully extended.
  2. The palm faces forward; the other hand can hold your waist or an upright for stability.
  3. Bend the elbow to lower the dumbbell behind the head in a controlled arc.
  4. Keep the upper arm vertical and close to the head throughout.
  5. Extend the elbow to return the dumbbell overhead.
  6. Complete all reps on one side, then switch.

Form tips

  • Only the forearm moves — the upper arm stays glued in place.
  • Don't let the torso lean to the side; this is also a core exercise.
