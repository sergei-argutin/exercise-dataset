Barbell Ab Rollout

A kneeling ab rollout using a loaded barbell with plates, giving smoother rolling and adjustable load compared to an ab wheel.

Core · Barbell · Intermediate

How to do the Barbell Ab Rollout

  1. Load plates of equal size on both ends of a barbell and place it on the floor.
  2. Kneel behind the bar and grip it shoulder-width, arms extended.
  3. Brace the core hard, then roll the bar forward away from your body.
  4. Go as far as you can keep a neutral spine — stop before the hips sag.
  5. Pull the bar back to the starting position by contracting the abs and lats.
  6. Repeat for the recommended number of repetitions.

Form tips

  • Start with small plates for a limited range; graduate to 45s for full rollouts.
  • The moment the lower back arches or drops, stop — you've exceeded your range.
