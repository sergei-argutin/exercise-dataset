Air Bike

A full-body conditioning exercise on the air bike, combining pedaling with an arm push-pull for sustained power output.

Also called: assault bike, fan bike, rogue echo bike, schwinn airdyne.

How to do the Air Bike

  1. Sit on the air bike and grip the moving handles.
  2. Place your feet on the pedals and brace your core.
  3. Push and pull the handles while pedaling at a steady pace.
  4. Drive with both arms and legs to maintain power output.
  5. Continue for the desired duration or distance.

Form tips

  • Keep your torso upright and avoid slumping over the handles.
  • Breathe rhythmically to sustain effort over longer intervals.
  • Pull the handles with your back, not just your arms.
