Floor EZ-Bar Press

A floor press variation using an EZ-bar for a more comfortable wrist and elbow position.

Chest · EZ Curl Bar · Intermediate

Equipment

EZ Curl Bar

Also called: ez bar, ez curl bar, curl bar, w-bar, cambered bar, zig-zag bar.

How to do the Floor EZ-Bar Press

  1. Lie on the floor holding an EZ-bar over your chest.
  2. Lower the bar until your elbows touch the floor.
  3. Press back up to full extension.
  4. Repeat.

Form tips

  • The EZ-bar reduces wrist strain vs. a straight bar.
  • Keep your feet flat on the floor.
