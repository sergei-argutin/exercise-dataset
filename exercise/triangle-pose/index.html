Triangle Pose

A wide-stance pose with both legs straight and the torso tilting sideways over the front leg, lengthening the hamstring and the whole side body.

Upper legs · Bodyweight · Intermediate

Muscles worked by the Triangle Pose

Primary

HamstringsHamstring MusclesObliquesOblique Abdominals

Secondary

AdductorsHip AdductorsGlute MediusGluteus Medius

Also called: hamstring, hamstrings, posterior thigh, biceps femoris, oblique, obliques.

Equipment

No equipment — this is a bodyweight exercise.

How to do the Triangle Pose

  1. Stand wide with the front foot turned out and the back foot angled slightly in.
  2. Keep both legs straight and reach the front hand forward as the hips shift back.
  3. Tilt sideways and bring the bottom hand to the shin, a block or the floor, top arm reaching up.
  4. Hold the position for the prescribed time, turning the chest towards the ceiling.
  5. Press into the feet to come up and repeat on the other side.

Form tips

  • Reach out over the front leg before you tilt down; folding early collapses the pose.
  • Use a block under the bottom hand rather than dragging the chest towards the floor.
  • Keep both legs straight and the kneecaps lifted.
