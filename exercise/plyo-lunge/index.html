Plyo Lunge

An explosive split-stance jump that switches the legs in mid-air, alternating each rep.

Upper legs · Bodyweight · Intermediate

Muscles worked by the Plyo Lunge

Primary

GlutesGluteus MaximusQuadsQuadriceps Femoris

Secondary

Also called: glute, glutes, gluteus maximus, buttocks, butt, quad.

Equipment

No equipment — this is a bodyweight exercise.

How to do the Plyo Lunge

  1. Start in a forward lunge with one foot ahead of the other.
  2. Drop into a deep lunge with the back knee almost touching the floor.
  3. Drive both feet off the floor, switching legs in mid-air.
  4. Land softly in a lunge with the opposite leg forward.
  5. Continue alternating without pausing.

Form tips

  • Land softly with bent knees to absorb impact.
  • Switch the leg position fully — do not just hop in place.
  • Keep the torso upright — do not pitch forward.
