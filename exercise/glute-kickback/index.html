Glute Kickback

A bodyweight glute isolation exercise performed on all fours to develop gluteus maximus strength and shape.

Upper legs · Bodyweight · Beginner

Muscles worked by the Glute Kickback

Primary

GlutesGluteus Maximus

Secondary

Lower BackErector SpinaeGlute MediusGluteus MediusHamstringsHamstring Muscles

Also called: glute, glutes, gluteus maximus, buttocks, butt.

Equipment

No equipment — this is a bodyweight exercise.

How to do the Glute Kickback

  1. Begin on all fours with hands under shoulders and knees under hips.
  2. Keeping the knee bent at 90 degrees, drive one heel toward the ceiling.
  3. Squeeze the glute at the top of the movement.
  4. Lower the leg back to the starting position with control.
  5. Complete all reps, then switch sides.

Form tips

  • Keep your hips square to the floor — avoid rotating.
  • Focus on squeezing the glute, not just lifting the leg.
  • Add a resistance band or cable for progressive overload.
